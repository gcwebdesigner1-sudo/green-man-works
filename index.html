How it works

  1. He drops it off

    Call or text with your address. Reserve before noon and same-day delivery may be available. The bag goes on your driveway or lawn, within 8 feet of a clear curb, so you skip the city street permit.

  2. You fill it

    No deadline and no daily fee. Clean out the garage this weekend or a little at a time over a month. Low 3 foot walls make it easy to swing a toilet or a stack of drywall over the side.

  3. The crane lifts it

    Text when it's full. The truck parks on the street and the crane picks the bag straight up onto a flatbed. Nothing heavy ever rolls onto your concrete.

  4. It's gone

    The load gets hauled off and your driveway looks like nothing happened. You get real-time texts with an ETA for both the drop-off and the pickup.

        Questions people ask

        How much does a dumpster rental cost in Lehi?

        $150 for the first 3.5 yard dumpster bag, including delivery, crane pickup and up to 3,500 lb. Each extra bag at the same address is $80. There's no daily fee. Heavy materials like dirt and concrete carry extra dump fees.

        How long can I keep the dumpster?

        As long as you want. There's no deadline. Fill it when you're ready and text when it's full.

        Where does the bag go?

        On your driveway or front lawn, within 8 feet of a curb with nothing in the way. Cars, trees or fences between the bag and the street block the crane. Keeping it off the street means no city permit.

        Will it damage my driveway?

        No. The bag is soft woven material and the truck stays on the street, so there are no steel rails or tires on your concrete.

        How many dumpsters can I rent?

        There's no limit. Extra bags at the same address are $80 because the delivery and dispatch cost is only paid once.

        Can I put dirt, concrete or tile in it?

        Yes. Tile and sharp debris are fine, the bags are tear resistant. Dirt is limited to about 2 yards of clean topsoil per bag, and dirt, concrete, rock, brick and gravel have extra dump fees.

        What if something is too big for the bag?

        Set it next to the bag and fill the bag with everything else. The oversized item gets priced by how much room is left.

        Can I get same-day delivery?

        Reserve before noon and same-day delivery in Utah County may be available. You'll get a text with the arrival time.

        What are your hours?

        Every day, 9 AM to 5 PM. Texts are the fastest way to reach him.
